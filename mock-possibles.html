Mock, 24 Sep · Reservations, All. No database behind it. The board above is tally.html's own CSS.
  • All off is the list exactly as it is today.
  • All on adds every villa that is in house tonight and has not answered dinner, in villa order among the confirmed ones. Declined and vacant villas never appear.
  • A possible is grey: the name, Awaiting where the covers go, and its dietaries in grey pills, solid for an allergy and tinted for a preference, the same shapes as the red ones.
  • The dietaries are the guest's own for the stay (their form, or whatever the desk or this board recorded), so what showed on the night they confirmed shows on every night after.
  • Tiles, Covers and the table make-up do not change: they count confirmed diners only.
  • The phone remembers whether All was last left on.
  • + Add reservation is renamed Add +.
