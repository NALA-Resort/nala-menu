About this mock

Step 1 only reads the units. Invented readings for a cool, humid Friday: before dawn it is 21° and 95% outside, and units set to 23 in Cool have stopped running, so they have stopped drying.

Green: humidity 65% or under. Amber: over 65%. Red words: the unit has gone offline. The line under each number is the last 24 hours of humidity. Tap a villa for its charts; touch a chart to read any moment.
