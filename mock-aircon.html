Air con board

About this mock

Invented readings for a cool, humid Friday: the morning, when units set to 23 in Cool have stopped running and stopped drying, and the warm afternoon. The board answers six questions, a row each; tap a row and the villas answer it: green nothing to do, amber chase this, dashed nothing to ask, red words offline.

Tap a villa for its controls and the humidity rule, Select to change several at once, Humidity rule for its settings.
