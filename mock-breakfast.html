How it works

  • On it: everyone who slept here last night, the guests on last night’s FOH Sheet, checkouts included. Today’s arrivals are not.
  • Two taps: the villa, then its table. Or the other way round: a free table, then the villa.
  • The tables are laid out as the room is: 41-48 on the left with odds beside evens, 61 and 62 in the middle, 51-58 on the right. Always in the same place, in the picker too. Green is taken, with the villa on it; white is free.
  • Seating a villa that stays on stamps Possibly available on Cleans at the time they sat down. It is the same mark as the board’s own button, so its clock, its order (seated longest first) and its push to housekeeping all work as they do now.
  • Tap a seated villa, or its table: another table moves them, Finished frees the table, Undo seating takes it back and asks first.

Calls I made

  • A villa checking out is a clean, and Cleans decides a clean by Departed, never by a glance (STYLEGUIDE, Services only). So seating 2, 5, 7, 10 or 12 marks nothing there: villa 7 is seated and still Occupied on the board.
  • Finished frees the table and leaves the Cleans mark alone. Its clock already ages it: amber at 15 minutes, red at 20.
  • Moving tables keeps the time they sat down. Undo takes back only the mark breakfast made, never one housekeeping set by hand.
  • An allergy is grey until they sit down and red once they do: Reservations’ All list does the same.
  • Two villas can share a table: the Whitfields, 3 and 4, at one.

Questions for you

  1. When a table finishes, should Cleans hear it? The guest may be walking back. I left it out.
  2. Do the tables have sizes or areas worth showing, a two-top or the deck? The picker could say so.
  3. Do outside guests come for breakfast? If so, an Add + for a walk-in.
  4. Once this is live, does the FOH Sheet’s B’fast table column go?
