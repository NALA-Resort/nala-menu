# Nala app: the housekeeping sheet

One screen. It opens straight to it when you sign in.

---

## The board

Every villa is a tile, and each carries the job for today:

- **CLEAN** - the guest leaves today. Full turnaround.
- **SERVICE** - the guest is staying on. Service only.
- **PRE-ARRIVAL** - nobody in it, somebody arriving. Ready it.
- **VACANT** - nothing to do.
- **UNKNOWN** - the app cannot tell from the dates. Check with the manager
  rather than guessing.

The job comes from the booking dates. **The manager can set it by hand and
their choice wins**, because they know things the dates do not. You cannot set
it yourself, and that is not a lack of trust: it is so two people cannot
decide the same villa differently on the same morning.

---

## Colour means "you can start now"

- **Blue** - ready to clean, the guest has gone.
- **Green** - ready to service.
- **White** - occupied, not ready.
- **Grey** - finished.

A pre-arrival never takes a colour, because nobody has to leave first. It is
always ready.

---

## Your four marks

- **Done** - the work is finished. This is the one everyone else is waiting
  for, and it tells the manager and the floor straight away.
- **Breakfast / available** - the villa looks free after breakfast.
- **Departed** - the guest has gone.
- **Push to tomorrow** - it is not getting done today. Better said than
  silently left.

Every one of them can be undone. Undoing never notifies anybody, because
nothing has become available by taking something back.

---

## Stripped and fresh linen

Two small toggles beside the villa's name on a clean's sheet, for a helper
working ahead of the cleaners:

- **Trolley** - the villa is stripped, the old linen is off.
- **Folded stack** - the fresh linen is on.

Tap to set, tap again to undo. Light grey means not done, dark grey means
done. The toggles only appear once the guest has gone - the villa is marked
departed, or it was pushed - because a villa cannot be stripped around its
guest. A set mark also appears in the top-left corner of the villa's tile, so
whoever takes the clean can see from the board that part of the job is
already finished. Nobody is notified either way - it is a note on the villa,
not an announcement.

---

## The clock beside an available villa

- **green** under ten minutes
- **ink** from ten
- **amber** from fifteen
- **red** from twenty

Red means the guest could walk back in. It is not a telling-off, it is the
board asking whether that villa should go to the front of your list.

---

## Two things to know about the numbers

**The order of the tiles is deliberate.** Services come first, because they
are attempted while the guest is out at breakfast, before the departure cleans
open up. Then cleans, then unknowns, then vacant, and villa order within each
block. Finished villas sink below outstanding ones. The top of your screen is
always the next thing to do.

**A villa that shows as unknown is missing information, not idle.** Say so.
It usually means a booking never reached the app, and you are the first person
in a position to notice.
