The sets

B Phosphor, the fill weight: rounded and even, the nearest to the phone's own filled tab icons, which is why it is suggested. A real lotus and a desk bell among 9,000 icons, in six weights.

C Material Symbols, Google's, rounded and filled: Android's look. The most literal set for a resort: its Spa icon is a lotus and its Front Desk icon is the room service bell.

D Lucide: line icons, the look of most new websites. Apple asks for filled icons in a tab bar, which is why A, B and C are filled. Lucide has no lotus, so Spa is a flower.
