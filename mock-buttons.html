2 · What a press gives back today
  • Nothing, on six surfaces. Every save on front-desk, tally (the sheet's Save details, Dining, Cancel booking and all six bulk actions), cleaners (about 40 buttons), spa, staff's permission ticks and index writes to Firebase with no disable, no label change, no colour, no spinner. The shared .btn has no :active rule: pressed and idle are the same pixels. A slow connection shows nothing, and a double tap writes twice.
  • Already right, in five corners: templates.html (Save · Saving · Saved, resting label says the record is clean), arrivals-sms and invitations (armed red confirm, live count, Sending), flags and tag (disable plus a Saved chip), publish (Publishing, then a done panel), prearrival (Sending). Each written locally, none shared.
  • Failure is the only feedback that exists broadly: seven different page-local error bars, one per page, error-only.

6 · What it costs
  • nala-ui.css: the 8px radius, the softened-ink resting solid (one new token), the :active press states, .saving and .saved. One commit, every page's ?v= bumped with it.
  • nala-shared.js: one helper - saveFeedback(btn, promise) - that disables, relabels, flashes green or reverts with the red line. The five pages that already do this right adopt it instead of their own copies; the six silent surfaces call it where they write.
  • Per page, as each is next touched (the standing rule for the destructive dress already): swap the local dress for the shared one, wire the helper. staff, stats, menu-print, past-menus and welcome drop their private .btn copies.
  • Guest pages keep the brand - Raleway, the brown - and adopt only the states.
Ruled by the owner · 27 Aug
  • Radius: 8px on free-standing buttons; the footer row keeps its corner law.
  • tally's choice pair keeps its colours - they preview the tile colour they set - rounded to 8px with everything else.
  • Saved holds until there is more to save, everywhere a surface stays open after a write.
  • The resting solid is softened ink, so the press darkens to full ink with visible contrast.
