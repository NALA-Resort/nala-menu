Pre-arrival, Dining and Spa colour the pill — they are the guest’s status. Clean is the room’s status, so its screen leaves the pills grey and speaks through a dot by the room number. A room that needs turning around for an arrival within 2 days flags its row amber (villas 2, 4, 8, 16). Rooms 15 and 17 need cleaning too, but their next arrival is 3–4 days off — quiet dot, no flag. An occupied room is never flagged: villas 1, 3, 5 and 12 have guests in house, so they stay calm whatever their clean state.
