What the database holds

One line here is one card in the world. Cut a card and a line appears, wipe one and its line goes, Mark lost flags it, and a line still here past its expiry is the Tally. The screens above are these lines, counted - nothing else. (Each record also keeps the card number the encoder reported, which is how the reader names a held card; it stays off every screen.)
