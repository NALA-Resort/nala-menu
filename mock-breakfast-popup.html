The front desk tablet held sideways, 1280 by 720: Sat 26th Sep at 08:12, with the demo guests. The earlier shape, the tables always on the right, is mock-breakfast.html. On a phone it is drawn smaller; turn the phone sideways for a bigger one.

How it works

  • One screen, nothing to scroll: the villas as the FOH Sheet lists them, in two columns, 1 to 9 and 10 to 17, each row a ninth of the screen high.
  • Each row: the villa, its table right beside it, the guest over their party and the day they leave, then the tags: the group, the flags, the dietaries.
  • Tap a villa’s line: the room’s tables pop up. Tap a table and they are seated; the pop-up shuts. Two taps.
  • Tap a seated villa: the same pop-up, their table marked. Another table moves them; Finished, Add a villa to table 44 and Undo seating are there too.
  • View tables, in the eighteenth place under villa 17, shows the room: tap a free table to seat a villa at it, or a taken one to see who is there, finish them, or add another. It says how many are free.
  • After the table, a villa staying on asks Available for service?, however the table was picked: from its row, or from View tables. Yes marks it on Cleans (the board’s own Possibly available) and puts a broom in its table cell; Not yet leaves it. Its pop-up can mark it later, shows when it was marked, and clears it, asking first.
  • Every field the app holds on a booking is in the demo: in the row the second guest, party, the day they leave, the group, the flags, the special occasion, the dietaries (or a declared none) and a notes bubble, amber when a dietary note waits; in the pop-up all of it in full, with the dietary note, the guest’s note, the booking note and tonight’s dinner.

Calls I made

  • Every villa keeps its row, so villa 12 is always in the same place; a villa nobody slept in fades in its row, and nothing re-sorts.
  • Checked with text drawn a seventh wider, as your phone drew it, and with a full house of seventeen: nothing spills. A row whose tags need a third line takes it, and the rest give up a pixel.
  • The free tables’ count on the View tables button: the one number left on the screen, and it costs no height.
  • A villa checking out is a clean, decided by Departed (STYLEGUIDE, Services only), so it is not asked: 2, 5, 7, 10 and 12.
  • Available for service is its own answer: undoing a seat or Finished leaves it as it was, and Clear is the way off. A villa already marked is not asked again, and a move to another table is not asked. In View tables, a villa answered Not yet keeps Available? beside its name on the table.
  • Not shown, as none of it is breakfast’s business: arrival time, the purpose of the stay, how often they plan to dine, treatments. Say if you want any.
  • An allergy is grey until they sit down, red once they do.
