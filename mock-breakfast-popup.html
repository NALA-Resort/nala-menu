The front desk tablet held sideways, 1280 by 720: Sat 26th Sep at 08:12, with the demo guests. The other shape, the tables always on the right, is mock-breakfast.html. On a phone it is drawn smaller; turn the phone sideways for a bigger one.

How it works

  • One screen, nothing to scroll: the villas across the whole of it, three to a row, every one in its own place.
  • Tap a villa: the room’s tables pop up. Tap a table and they are seated; the pop-up shuts. Two taps.
  • Tap a seated villa: the same pop-up, their table marked. Another table moves them; Finished, Add a villa to table 44 and Undo seating are there too.
  • View tables, in the eighteenth place, shows the room: tap a free table to seat a villa at it, or a taken one to see who is there, finish them, or add another. Its button says how many are free.
  • Seating a villa that stays on stamps Possibly available on Cleans at the time they sat down; the broom on the bar shows the board.

Calls I made

  • Cards three to a row, 1 to 17, as the Cleans board reads, with View tables where Cleans keeps Select multiple.
  • The free tables’ count on the View tables button: the one number left on the screen, and it costs no height.
  • A villa checking out is a clean, decided by Departed (STYLEGUIDE, Services only): seating 2, 5, 7, 10 or 12 marks nothing on Cleans.
  • Finished frees the table and leaves the Cleans mark to age on its own clock. An allergy is grey until they sit down, red once they do.
