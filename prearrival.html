Where do you plan on eating during your stay?
No right answer. It only helps us plan.
Our restaurant creates menus and meals for the number of guests onsite. Knowing how often you plan on eating with us means less waste, and a better experience on the nights that you do.
Will you be dining with us on your first night?
Dining is from 6:00 to 6:30.
Dinner is a set menu, written by the kitchen on the day, from whatever is best that morning. That means the dishes for your first night do not exist yet. We will send them to you as soon as they do.
Does anyone in your party have allergies or dietary requirements?
We would rather know now than on the night.
The menu is set each day, so a dish cannot be rebuilt around a requirement on the night. What we can do, and gladly do, is leave things out and cook something alongside instead. The earlier we know, the more room the kitchen has to work with.
