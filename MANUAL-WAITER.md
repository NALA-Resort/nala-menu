# Nala app: the waiter's sheet

Two screens, and a small job on a third.

---

## Reservations: who is coming, and what they cannot eat

Every villa is a tile.

- **Vacant** - nobody booked in.
- **Awaiting** - somebody is in it and has not answered yet.
- **In** - dining, party size on the tile.
- **Out** - not dining tonight.

**The speech bubble is the important one.** It carries dietaries and notes,
under two headings:

- *Tonight's dining notes* - given for tonight. Act on these.
- *Previous dining notes* - given on an earlier night of the same stay. **Ask
  before acting on them.** A dietary is an answer to one night's invitation.
  Somebody who avoided dairy on Monday has not necessarily asked to avoid it
  tonight, and assuming they did is how a table gets the wrong plate.

A red bar down the side of a booking means the guest's dietary clashes with
something on tonight's menu. That is worth knowing before you take the table,
not while you are standing at it.

You can edit a booking: party size, dietaries, notes, seating a walk-in. What
you set outranks the guest's own link, so if you have just spoken to them,
what you enter wins.

## Cleans: one job, and one only

You are on that board to say a villa **looks available** after you have
cleared breakfast, and to mark a **departure** when you see one.

You will not see buttons for marking a clean done, or for pushing a villa to
tomorrow. That is housekeeping's work and it is hidden rather than greyed out,
so nothing gets pressed by accident.

**Why you and not them:** clearing breakfast means you are in the villa, or
outside it, before anybody else knows the guest has gone. Marking it starts
the clock, and housekeeping is told straight away.

**The timer that appears next to it:**

- **green** under ten minutes
- **ink** from ten
- **amber** from fifteen
- **red** from twenty - the guest is coming back and the villa is not ready

You can take a departure mark back if you were wrong. Undoing does not notify
anybody, because nothing has become available by undoing something.
