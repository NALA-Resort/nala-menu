What it does

  • A text to each guest the day before a booked treatment, saying what is booked and when.
  • The desk sends it, from a new page: SMS › Spa reminders. Nothing goes out on its own.
  • The Dashboard gains a Spa reminders card for tomorrow's treatments, a door to that page. Today's Spa bookings card is unchanged.

Calls made - say if any is wrong

  • The day before, not the morning of: a 9:00 am treatment can only be reminded the day before. A treatment booked for the same day gets no text; that guest has only just booked it.
  • The date, not “tomorrow”. “Sunday 27 September” is right whatever time the text goes out.
  • One text per treatment. A pair is one text to the booker.
  • Changed after its text (day, time or length): back to To send in amber, and the next text adds “(changed from 11:30 am)”. Cancelled after its text: no automatic text; the Spa board's cancel already says the guest must be told.
  • Not in the text: the price, where the treatment happens, a link. Any of them is a line in the template or one more placeholder.
  • Replies land on the Guest Touch mobile, like replies to the other two texts.
  • Length: plain SMS characters only; a middot or a curly quote would triple the cost. Gentle reminder is 2 segments, Short is 1.
